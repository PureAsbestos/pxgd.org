PxGd is a pixel art editor made by [PureAsbestos](https://pureasbestos.com) with Godot. It offers first-class support for mobile devices.
It is currently still in the early stages of development. There is [a devlog](https://youtu.be/dcojTX6x5qU) you can watch about it.
